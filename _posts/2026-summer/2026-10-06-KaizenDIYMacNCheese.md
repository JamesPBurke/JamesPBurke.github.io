# The Result

This recipe finished out my calories for the day *and* my protein. It's a keeper, as long as I'm willing to take the calorie hit from all the fat. But if you're craving cheese, it's a decent trade-off. The 35g+ of total fat is a lot. A leaner version of this maybe uses only one slice of American cheese, or increases the grated cheese and replaces some of the cheddar with fat free cottage cheese. Something I will try when I'm not in a hurry.

# DIY Protein Mac and Cheese

**Yield:** 1 serving  
**Cook time:** About 10 minutes

## Ingredients

- 2 slices American cheese
- 1 ounce sharp white cheddar cheese
- 1 tablespoon grated Parmesan cheese
- 1/2 tablespoon butter
- 2 tablespoons 1% milk
- 2 ounces Kaizen protein pasta
- Salted water, for boiling
- 1 teaspoon salt, or to taste

## Instructions

1. Bring the salted water to a boil. Add the pasta and boil for 5 minutes.
2. While the pasta cooks, melt the American, cheddar, and Parmesan cheeses together over very low heat.
3. When the cheeses soften, stir in the butter and milk until a sauce forms.
4. Drain the pasta and add it to the cheese sauce. Stir until evenly coated.
5. Add salt to taste, but there's already a lot of sodium here, so you might want to skip it.
6. Serve immediately.
