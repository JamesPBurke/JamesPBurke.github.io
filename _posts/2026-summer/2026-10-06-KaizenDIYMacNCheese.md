# DIY Protein Mac and Cheese

**Yield:** 1 serving  
**Cook time:** About 10 minutes

## Ingredients

- 2 slices American cheese
- 1 ounce sharp white cheddar cheese
- 1/4 cup shredded Parmesan cheese
- 1/2 tablespoon butter
- 2 tablespoons 1% milk
- 2 ounces Kaizen protein pasta
- Salted water, for boiling
- 1 teaspoon salt, or to taste

## Instructions

1. Bring the salted water to a boil. Add the pasta and boil for 5 minutes.
2. While the pasta cooks, melt the American, cheddar, and Parmesan cheeses together over very low heat.
3. When the cheeses soften, stir in the butter and milk until a sauce forms.
4. Drain the pasta and add it to the cheese sauce. Stir until evenly coated.
5. Add salt to taste.
6. Serve immediately.

## Nutrition Notes

- Values are estimates because nutrition varies by cheese brand, slice weight, and the specific Kaizen pasta variety.
- The estimate assumes standard 21-gram American-cheese slices and a 2-ounce serving of Kaizen pasta.
- Kaizen pasta contributes approximately 130 calories, 20 grams protein, 21 grams carbohydrate, and 15 grams fiber per 2-ounce serving ([Kaizen product data](https://kaizenfoodcompany.com/pages/product-information); [full nutrition listing](https://www.vitaminshoppe.com/p/protein-fusilli-pasta-8-oz-piece-s/kzz-17615)).
- One teaspoon of table salt contributes roughly 2,300 milligrams of sodium ([Montana State University Extension](https://www.montana.edu/extension/buyeatlivebetter/other_nep_resources/fact_sheets/sodium/Factsheet_Sodium_.pdf)). Consider starting with 1/8 teaspoon and adjusting after tasting.
- Allergens: contains milk. Kaizen pasta contains lupin and fava bean ingredients; check the package if you have legume or peanut allergies.
