When we were kids there was this crazy show on TV called _Batman_. It was about a guy[^1] who put on a silly-looking costume and got into fistfights with even sillier villains with weird accents and mannerisms. And their henchmen who wore odd uniforms that made them look like doofuses. Like adult versions of Dr. Seuss's Thing 1 and Thing 2 from The Cat in the Hat. 

[^1]: Batman

And it wasn't a cartoon. It was live action. So, it was an adult guy wearing tights. And his "ward," who was a slightly younger guy pretending to be a teenager, also wearing tights. This younger guy was "Robin." I guess he was adopted? His parents were deceased. Which was also true of Batman. In fact, this was kind of an important connection in the lore. Which, to my memory, they never really mentioned. 

They mentioned other things. There was a butler. Did I mention that Batman, uh, I mean the guy who dressed up like Batman -- not the actor, but the character of the guy in the show who liked to dress in tights and punch people -- was rich? Yeah. He inherited a bunch of cash from his dead parents. 

So they mentioned that. And they also mentioned that he had a bat cave. A hideout crossed with a secret crime lab crossed with a garage for his bat car. Which was actually a bat_mobile_. Like the Pope Mobile, only for bats. Or people who dressed vaguely like a bat. Only this predated the Pope Mobile, because this TV show was before kooks were taking pot shots at the Pope.

There was a bat pole. Don't get confused. Bats don't have a pole in real life. A _Batman_ has a bat pole. And so does a Robin. It worked like this: he (Batman) got a call on the bat phone from the police commissioner.
