## Resources

### OSHA Standards

[29 CFR 1910.212 — General Requirements for All Machines](https://www.osha.gov/laws-regs/regulations/standardnumber/1910/1910.212)

The baseline machine guarding rule: one or more methods of guarding to protect operators and others from hazards such as point of operation, rotating parts, flying chips, and sparks.

[29 CFR 1910.213 — Woodworking Machinery Requirements](https://www.osha.gov/laws-regs/regulations/standardnumber/1910/1910.213)

Machine-specific guarding requirements for table saws, band saws, radial saws, jointers, and other woodworking equipment, including anti-kickback devices and spreaders.

[29 CFR 1910.215 — Abrasive Wheel Machinery](https://www.osha.gov/laws-regs/regulations/standardnumber/1910/1910.215)

Requirements for bench and pedestal grinders: work rest set within 1/8", tongue guard within 1/4", safety guard coverage, and wheel ring-testing and mounting.

### OSHA eTools

[Woodworking eTool](https://www.osha.gov/etools/woodworking)

Interactive guidance on kickback, riving knives and spreaders, blade guards, and safe operating practices for common woodshop machines.

[Machine Guarding eTool](https://www.osha.gov/etools/machine-guarding)

Explains the hazardous motions and actions common to machinery and walks through guard types (fixed, interlocked, adjustable, self-adjusting) and devices.

### Shop Safety / Machine Guarding

[Georgia Tech OSHA Consultation Program — Machine Guarding Training Materials](https://oshainfo.gatech.edu/resources/training-materials/machine-guarding/)

Free lesson plans, handouts, and slide decks (developed under an OSHA Susan Harwood Targeted Training Grant) covering machine guarding case studies, general and introductory machine guarding, machine guarding awareness, grinder safety, mechanical power presses, robotics safety, woodworking safety, and lockout/tagout — most run about an hour, several with Spanish-language versions.

[OSHA Susan Harwood Grant Materials — by Topic](https://www.osha.gov/harwoodgrants/grantmaterials/bytopic)

Searchable database of grantee-produced training products — slide decks, handouts, and lesson plans — organized by hazard topic, including machine guarding, woodworking, and amputations.

[Power Tool Institute — Training Resources](https://www.powertoolinstitute.com/resources/training-resources/)

Educator-oriented lesson plans, quizzes, and videos on safe use of portable and stationary power tools, produced by the industry association of power tool manufacturers.

### Young Worker Safety / Talking Safety

[NIOSH — Talking Safety](https://www.cdc.gov/niosh/young-workers/talking-safety/)

The national hub for Talking Safety, a free foundational occupational safety and health curriculum for middle and high school students. Six lessons cover recognizing hazards, assessing and controlling risk, and knowing worker rights, with state-specific versions available for all 50 states plus DC.

[Massachusetts — Youth Work: Talking Safety](https://www.mass.gov/info-details/youth-work-talking-safety)

The Massachusetts landing page for the state-specific Talking Safety curriculum, with downloadable lesson plans, student handouts, and slides tailored to Massachusetts youth employment and safety law.

[NIOSH Youth@Work: Talking Safety](https://www.cdc.gov/niosh/talkingsafety/)

The original NIOSH Youth@Work portal for the curriculum (redirects to the current Talking Safety pages), with background on the program and links to every state version.
